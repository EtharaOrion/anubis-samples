settlement. The figures below were published with it. Exactly one reading of the
material reproduces every one of them.

- C1 matches opened: 5
- C2 records recovered: 238
- C3 distinct squads: 16
- C4 gross output: 6228
- C5 voided output: 1703
- C6 raw upkeep: 8166
- C7 charged upkeep: 7939
- C8 overflow upkeep: 227
- C9 champion entrants: 1
- C10 qualifier entrants: 1
- C11 provisional entrants: 14

A reading that reproduces the first six figures has pinned the guard quantities.
A reading that then reproduces the next two has pinned the season budget. A
reading that then reproduces the last three has pinned the promotion threshold.
